A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Little questions.
Honest answers.

Can Moku see what’s on my screen?

Moku reads the text in the window you’re working in, with the app’s name, the window title and the web address, right on your Mac. The screen image is never saved or sent anywhere. To tell work from a detour, the app, the window title, the web address and a short excerpt of that text go to an AI model with zero data retention, through our relay, which doesn’t keep them either. He never reads the text in password managers, authenticator apps or crypto wallets. Your days and houses stay on your Mac. Moku counts how often you type, never which keys, and never records audio. Take a break and he stops watching until you’re back.

Read the privacy policy
What if I have a very unproductive day?

Then you had a very human day. A few floorboards still count. Your day keeps a record of what you built, even if the house isn’t finished. Tomorrow brings a fresh plot, and Moku will be there with you.

When can I bring Moku home?

Right now. Moku is in beta for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later. Download him, drag him into Applications, and he’ll say hi.
